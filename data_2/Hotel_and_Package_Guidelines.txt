SKYTRAIL HOLIDAYS
HOTEL AND PACKAGE GUIDELINES

1. STANDARD HOTEL TIMES
Unless the hotel voucher states otherwise, standard check-in begins at 14:00 and standard check-out is by 11:00 local time. Early check-in and late check-out are requests only and may involve a hotel charge.

2. HOTEL GUARANTEE AND NO-SHOW
A prepaid hotel booking is guaranteed for the arrival date shown on the voucher. A traveller arriving after 20:00 local time should inform the hotel. Failure to arrive without notice may be treated as a no-show and may result in the first night or the full stay becoming non-refundable, depending on the supplier rule.

3. PACKAGE DEPOSIT
A package reservation requires a deposit of £150 per traveller. The remaining balance is due 30 calendar days before departure. A booking made within 30 days of departure requires full payment at confirmation.

4. PACKAGE CANCELLATION
A cancellation submitted more than 30 calendar days before departure normally loses the deposit only. A cancellation 15 to 30 calendar days before departure may be charged 50 percent of the package price. A cancellation fewer than 15 calendar days before departure may be charged 100 percent. Supplier-specific terms shown on the confirmation take priority.

5. HOTEL OR PACKAGE CHANGES
Changing the traveller name, destination, hotel, room type, or travel date requires supplier review. Price differences and service fees may apply.

6. DOCUMENT RESPONSIBILITY
Travellers are responsible for valid passports, visas, permits, and entry requirements. The agency can direct the traveller to official authorities but cannot confirm that a person will be admitted to a country.
